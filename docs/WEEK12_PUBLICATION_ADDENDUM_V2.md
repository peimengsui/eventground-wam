## Evidence retained

This format decision follows inspection of the completed results. The same four
research questions and evidence remain available; editorial emphasis can favor
the implemented system, controlled outcomes and practical lessons. The five-arm
study establishes no overall coupling benefit, the exploratory event study
establishes no additional RLVR benefit, and formal adaptive integration loses
32 targeted and 10 clean successes net relative to original fixed M4. Later
exploratory work establishes no replacement for that Week 11 reference.

All panel, uncertainty and interpretation rules in the original scope continue
to apply. A selected gain and loss can illustrate the result but cannot measure
its prevalence. Single-seed evidence, panel reuse, input differences, the JEPA
qualification exception and weak event labels retain their relevant limits.

## Preparation boundary

Web adaptation may simplify diagrams, re-render charts from the verified
tables, and export clips from retained recordings. Derived assets must preserve
their source, case, timing and outcome provenance. Replaying saved frames for
video encoding is allowed; simulator replay, new model inference, training,
rollouts or new outcome labeling are outside this publication phase. Full-10K
and test panels remain locked.

The current request authorizes planning for personal publication. The plan
does not mark the article drafted or the website published. No website write,
upload or deployment is performed by this revision.
