Source: verified results package, November 1, 2026.
Publication labels updated November 4, 2026. Numerical values, evidence notes,

MODEL NAMES
B0: action-only policy.
M1: pixel auxiliary training; no future branch at deployment.
M2: latent/JEPA auxiliary training; no future branch at deployment.
M3: pixel-trained future tokens coupled to action refinement.
M4: latent-trained future tokens coupled to action refinement.
M4-fixed: original M4 with horizon 8 and no event inference.
M4-EG-fixed8: event inference runs, but the horizon stays 8.
M4-EG-adaptive: event onset selects the future horizon.

FILES
success_rates.csv: 29 rows across distinct robot studies and panels.
contrasts.csv: 36 paired/factorial effects with intervals, analysis units,
  evidence labels and source identities. Effects are percentage points.
event_forecasting.csv: 7 revised-task event-prediction summaries. This is
  agreement with automatic event targets, not deployed-policy accuracy.

READING THE STUDIES
Five-policy comparison: five arms, 1,000 perturbed cases and 2,000 clean episodes per arm.
  Perturbed uncertainty resamples variants. Clean uncertainty resamples 40
  canonical tasks, retaining all 50 states per task. The 1K panel includes
  development tasks. Input differences make affected comparisons descriptive.
Event forecasting: exploratory prediction on demonstrations. Development selection
  and report scoring are distinct. Report uncertainty resamples physical
  demonstration sources. Labels are automatic, with prior report exposure and
  unresolved independent label review. No RLVR robot-policy benefit was tested.
Adaptive-horizon evaluation: 467 targeted and 200 clean episodes per arm. Targeted intervals
  resample variants; clean intervals resample 40 tasks with five states each.
  This used a frozen protocol; the panels were not untouched test sets.
Continuation and motion-adapter screens: exploratory, often selected or reused cases. Keep the
  incomplete adapter screen and the fresh H8 task-cluster sensitivity distinct.
  These rows cannot be pooled into a larger independent benchmark denominator.

All intervals are conditional on the trained endpoints; they do not quantify
training-seed variability. The study retains one training seed, panel reuse,
historical source-overlap concerns, RGB/rendering differences, the latent
predictor's failed front-camera short-horizon check and its research-use
exception. Full-10K and test evaluations were not run for this reporting phase.

CSV values retain full precision. The article rounds effects to two decimal
places. Source fields use descriptive provenance identifiers, not public URLs.
The authoring package retains the exact mapping back to original research paths.
