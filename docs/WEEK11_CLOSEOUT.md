## What is established and what remains qualified

The original event-adaptive integration regresses on the tested formal panels.
Changing horizon changes the action policy's motion, and narrow interventions
show that these changes can help or harm particular trajectories. Gains in
acquisition or retention can coexist with lost recovery and wrong-fixture
engagement. The latest fresh comparison does not replicate H8's earlier
selected-case advantage. None of this proves that all event-adaptive designs
must fail, or that the small continuation comparison establishes equivalence
or general inferiority.

The final report must retain the single-training-seed and reused-panel limits,
the original JEPA failure/research-use exception, historical source-alias
exposure, renderer/input qualifications and lack of independent event-label QA.
Week 10's event-prediction scores do not establish on-policy event accuracy.
In the latest H8 comparison, 32 targeted variant cases cover 24 underlying
tasks; its supplementary task-grouped analysis preserves the negative decision.
M4 remains a reference for this phase, not a proven best model across the project.
